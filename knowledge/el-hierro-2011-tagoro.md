# Submarine eruption of El Hierro (Tagoro), 2011–2012

Sources: Wikipedia, “2011–12 El Hierro eruption” (accessed 26-10-2026); IGN, seismotectonics of El Hierro; IGN earthquake catalogue.

- **17 Jul 2011**: the seismic crisis begins under El Hierro.
- **20–24 Jul 2011**: more than 400 earthquakes. By 25 Aug 2011 there have been more than 4,000.
- **25 Aug 2011**: horizontal deformation is detected.
- **Late Sep 2011**: several families are evacuated.
- **10 Oct 2011, ~04:15 local time**: harmonic tremor appears. The eruption is **submarine**, on a fissure ~2 km south of **La Restinga**.
- **Early Nov 2011**: second evacuation of La Restinga (~600 residents).
- **Cone depth**: about 150–180 m below sea level at first, 100 m in Feb 2012 and 86 m by mid-Apr 2012.
- **5 Mar 2012**: the eruption is declared over.
- **Jun 2012**: a new deformation episode (4 cm in three days) without an eruption.
- The volcano was officially named **Tagoro** in 2016.

Lesson for VolScan: the crisis lasted almost **three months** before the eruption, with seismicity migrating from north to south. An unrest index would have stayed high for weeks without telling the exact moment. That is why VolScan does not give an “eruption probability”.
