# Tenerife: seismic unrest under Teide (2026)

Sources: Diario de Avisos, “Qué está pasando bajo el Teide” (7-8-2026) and related articles (July–August 2026); Sol del Sur, VULCANEX drill (22-10-2026). Accessed 26-10-2026.

- According to the IGN, seismo-volcanic activity has been **above normal since February 2026**.
- **7–27 Feb 2026**: seven swarms of **hybrid events** south-west of Pico Viejo, ~6.5–8 km below sea level. On 18 and 19 February there were ~950 events, all very small and not felt.
- **13–16 Mar 2026**: 59 earthquakes, magnitude < 1.8, 8–21 km deep.
- **July 2026**: 724 earthquakes in the Canaries, 611 of them in central Tenerife (Las Cañadas).
- Long-period signals have been recorded since 2016. There were similar swarms in 2016, 2019, 2022, 2024 and 2025.
- **Deformation**: GNSS and InSAR stations see no significant deformation in July. Since 2023, ~2 cm have accumulated in the centre of the island.
- **Official assessment (PEVOLCA / IGN)**: no signs of an eruption in the short or medium term. Small earthquakes could be felt, mainly in Las Cañadas. The volcanic traffic light has not changed.
- What would change the assessment: shallower and larger earthquakes, a sustained increase in seismicity, fast deformation or significant changes in gases.
- **22 Oct 2026**: **VULCANEX drill** on Tenerife (Guía de Isora and Adeje). The island council *simulated* an **orange** light. It was an **exercise**, not a real alert.
