# Historical eruptions in the Canary Islands

Source: Wikipedia, “List of volcanoes in Spain” (accessed 26-10-2026). Older dates are uncertain.

| Island | Eruption | Start | End |
|---|---|---|---|
| La Palma | Tacande or Montaña Quemada | 1430–1440 | ? |
| Tenerife | Eruption seen by Columbus | 1492 | ? |
| La Palma | Tehuya | 19 May 1585 | 10 Aug 1585 |
| La Palma | Martín or Tigalate | 2 Oct 1646 | 21 Dec 1646 |
| La Palma | San Antonio | 17 Nov 1677 | 21 Jan 1678 |
| Tenerife | Sietefuentes | 31 Dec 1704 | 4–5 Jan 1705 |
| Tenerife | Fasnia | 5 Jan 1705 | 16 Jan 1705 |
| Tenerife | Arafo | 2 Feb 1705 | 27 Mar 1705 |
| Tenerife | Garachico (Arenas Negras) | 5 May 1706 | 13 Jun 1706 |
| La Palma | El Charco | 9 Oct 1712 | 3 Dec 1712 |
| Lanzarote | Timanfaya | 1 Sep 1730 | 16 Apr 1736 |
| Tenerife | Narices del Teide (Chahorra) | 9 Jun 1798 | 14–15 Sep 1798 |
| Lanzarote | Tao (del Clérigo Duarte) | 31 Jul 1824 | 1824 |
| Lanzarote | Nuevo del Fuego (Chinero) | 29 Sep 1824 | 5 Oct 1824 |
| Lanzarote | Tinguatón | 10 Oct 1824 | 24 Oct 1824 |
| Tenerife | Chinyero | 18 Nov 1909 | 27 Nov 1909 |
| La Palma | San Juan (Duraznero, Hoyo Negro, Llano del Banco) | 24 Jun 1949 | 30 Jul 1949 |
| La Palma | Teneguía | 26 Oct 1971 | 18 Nov 1971 |
| El Hierro | Tagoro (submarine) | 10 Oct 2011 | 5 Mar 2012 |
| La Palma | Tajogaite | 19 Sep 2021 | 13 Dec 2021 |

Summary: La Palma has the most historical eruptions (8), followed by Tenerife (7, counting 1492), Lanzarote (4: Timanfaya and the three of 1824) and El Hierro (1, submarine). La Gomera, Gran Canaria and Fuerteventura have no historical eruptions.
